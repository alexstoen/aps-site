Privacy Policy

Aerial Professional Services LLC, including Alex Stoen Photography

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE]

This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, who processes it for us, and how to contact us about it. It applies to:

  • www.aerialprofessionalservices.com: drone photography and video services;
  • www.alexstoen.com: fine-art photography. Alex Stoen Photography is a registered trade name of Aerial Professional Services LLC;
  • our business email, quotes, orders and invoices;
  • the software we use to publish our own content to our Instagram accounts and Facebook Pages.

We are a small business in Colorado Springs, Colorado. "We" and "us" mean Aerial Professional Services LLC.

Information we collect

Quote form, aerialprofessionalservices.com. When you request a quote, we receive:

  • your first and last name;
  • your email address;
  • your phone number (optional);
  • the service you need;
  • a project address (optional);
  • your timeline;
  • your message.

The form is processed by Web3Forms, a form service, which emails it to us.

Enquiry form, alexstoen.com. When you send an enquiry, we receive:

  • the type of enquiry;
  • your name and email address;
  • optional details: organization, project location, number of artworks, timeline and artwork of interest;
  • your message.

Our own form service, which runs on Cloudflare, receives the enquiry and forwards it to our business email through Resend, an email-delivery service. To protect the form from spam and abuse, the service also keeps:

  • a one-way, salted code derived from your IP address (the IP address itself is not stored);
  • a similar code derived from technical details of your browser's request.

Email, texts and correspondence. When you email or text us, we receive your name, email address or phone number, and whatever the message and any attachments contain.

Clients and orders. When you hire us or buy a print, we keep what we need to do the work and run the business:

  • your name and contact details;
  • project or delivery address;
  • what you ordered;
  • quotes, invoices and payment records.

Website usage information. Our websites and their hosting providers collect technical and usage information, described under "Cookies and analytics".

How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • reply to enquiries and prepare quotes;
  • carry out and deliver the work you request, including producing and shipping fine-art prints;
  • invoice, receive payments and keep business and tax records;
  • understand, in aggregate, how our websites are used;
  • protect our websites and forms from spam and abuse;
  • meet our legal obligations.

We don't sell personal information. We don't share it with third parties for their own advertising or marketing. We don't add anyone to a promotional mailing list because they submitted a form.

Publishing to Instagram and Facebook

We use our own Meta developer app, "Business OS Publisher", only with our own accounts:

  • Instagram: @aerialprofessionalservices and @alexstoen;
  • Facebook Pages: Aerial Professional Services LLC and Alex Stoen Photography.

What Meta's permissions allow. The permissions Meta grants for our accounts allow the app to publish content and to read information about our accounts and posts. Depending on the permission, that can include comments or messages people send to our accounts.

What our app actually does. It is set up only to:

  • publish photographs and captions we've approved;
  • confirm each publication;
  • read summary figures about our own posts, such as like counts, comment counts and reach, and audience totals.

It doesn't offer "Log in with Facebook" to the public, and it doesn't use Meta data for advertising.

What it stores:

  • access keys Meta issues for our accounts: stored encrypted in our Cloudflare account;
  • each approved photo and caption: stored only until publication on each platform is complete;
  • a publishing record for each post: date, post identifiers and links;
  • performance figures: counts and totals only.

It doesn't store the names or profiles of people who view, like or comment on our posts, the text of their comments, or their messages. Comments and messages sent to our accounts stay on Instagram and Facebook, where Meta's policies apply.

How long we keep information

Retention depends on the type of record:

  • alexstoen.com enquiry-form service: records held on the form service itself are deleted automatically 30 days after they arrive. This applies only to that service. The email notification we receive becomes part of our business correspondence.
  • aerialprofessionalservices.com quote form: Web3Forms' published terms provide for submissions to be kept for up to three years. The email we receive becomes part of our business correspondence.
  • Email, client and order records, quotes, invoices and payment records: kept as long as needed to serve you, run the business, and meet tax, accounting and legal record-keeping obligations. We then delete them when they are no longer needed.
  • Website analytics and other service providers: held according to each provider's own settings and policies.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information:

  • our websites and forms use encrypted (HTTPS) connections;
  • our business accounts require sign-in;
  • publishing access keys are stored encrypted.

No transmission or storage method is completely secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.
