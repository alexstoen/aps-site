Construction Documentation

One flight a month.
A clear record of the whole build.

Owners and lenders want to see progress without driving to the site. Project managers need a visual record for schedule updates, draw requests and the occasional dispute. A monthly drone flight gives everyone the same view, on the same day, from the same positions.

We fly fixed waypoints each visit, so this month's images line up with last month's. You get a download link within 48 hours that you can forward to anyone on the project.

Every flight is flown by an FAA Part 107 certified remote pilot carrying $1M in liability coverage. Controlled-airspace authorizations near Peterson, Fort Carson and the Colorado Springs airport are requested before we arrive.
